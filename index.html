RLC Dynamics

Explore transient behavior in a coupled capacitor–inductor network.

Parameters

Transient response

Numerically calculated signals · zero initial stored energy

Circuit topology

R1 feeds node A. C1 connects A to ground; L1 and C3 connect A to B in parallel. C2 and R2 connect B to ground.

Original laboratory evidence

NI Multisim, multisim lab 2.pdf, Problem 1.3, Figures 1.7–1.11. These screenshots are distinct from the numerical results above.

Method and limitations

Kirchhoff current equations use the capacitance matrix [[C1+C3, −C3], [−C3, C2+C3]]. The inductor derivative is (VA−VB)/L. Fourth-order Runge–Kutta integration assumes ideal components, instantaneous pulse edges and zero initial conditions. Capacitor currents are positive from A or B toward ground, and from A to B for C3. Results are not measured data or a validated Multisim reproduction.
